# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want SlangImage to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone,
regardless of background or identity. Contributors and maintainers pledge to
make participation a respectful and constructive experience.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal or political attacks,
publishing others' private information, and other conduct that would reasonably
be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions) and when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1, as its standard of conduct. Instances of abusive, harassing, or
otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the maintainers by
opening a confidential
[security advisory](https://github.com/tekintatar/slangimage/security/advisories/new)
(the same private channel used for security reports). All complaints will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
